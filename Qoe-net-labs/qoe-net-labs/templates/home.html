Measure how a video call actually feels, even when the network drops out.

QoE-Net Labs runs a real two-party WebRTC call in your browser, reads the transport statistics every five seconds, and turns them into an estimated quality score you can compare with what participants report.

Start a conference Set up an experiment

Real measurements

Bitrate, frame rate, resolution, packet loss, jitter, round-trip time and freezes come straight from RTCPeerConnection.getStats(). Nothing is simulated.

Intermittent connectivity

Pause the sending side for a set time, once or on a schedule, and watch the receiving peer's stats react. Shape the real network with your OS tools for harsher tests.

Objective and subjective

Each call produces an estimated score per window. After the call, participants rate it 1–5 so you can check how well the estimate tracks perception.

Exportable

Every session, measurement and experiment can be downloaded as CSV or JSON for analysis in R, Python or a spreadsheet.
